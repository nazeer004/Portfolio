The Studio

Design with
intention

Founded in 2011, NAZEER is a boutique interior architecture studio built on the belief that truly exceptional spaces emerge from deep listening. We begin with silence — observing how light falls, how people move, what the architecture asks of us — before a single line is drawn.

Our team of seventeen works across residential, commercial, and hospitality typologies, with projects in Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia. We do not take on more than eight projects at a time.
