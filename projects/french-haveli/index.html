01 / The place

Not just a stay.
A sense of place.

In the old city, a home is never only a building. It is a courtyard, a neighbour's greeting, a shaded threshold and a story carried through generations.

French Haveli brings guests into the life of Ahmedabad's historic pol neighbourhoods. Its restored Gujarati home pairs traditional architectural character with the comforts of a small, welcoming heritage stay.

02 / A home with history

Where the
courtyard is the
heart of home.

At the centre of the haveli, the open court welcomes daylight and air. A traditional jhula offers a place to pause, while carved timber, patterned floors and everyday objects keep the character of the house close.

The home is part of a wider effort to help preserve Ahmedabad's built heritage and strengthen the community around it.
