01 / The studio

Design that
lives with you.

A space should look beautiful. But more importantly, it should feel right to live, work and spend time in.

Magic Space Designs is an Ahmedabad-based interior design studio led by founder and principal designer Ravi Rajpurohit. The studio works across residential, commercial, villa and hospitality interiors, bringing design thinking and execution together.

04 / The approach

From first thought
to final detail.

A clear sequence helps every decision feel considered — and keeps the project moving with purpose.

01 / Listen

Understand

We begin with your space, needs, preferences, practical requirements and budget.

02 / Imagine

Plan & design

Layouts, concepts and 3D visualisations help shape the direction before execution.

03 / Refine

Develop

Materials, colours, furniture and details are brought together into a cohesive plan.

04 / Realise

Execute

The approved design is carried into the space through coordinated project execution.
